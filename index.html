Grizzly Bear

The Grizzly Bears are powerful apex predators easily recognized by the large muscle hump on their shoulders, which gives them incredible digging power and the ability to sprint up to 35 miles per hour. Despite their fearsome reputation and long, four-inch claws, these massive mammals are actually omnivores whose diets consist mostly of berries, roots, and nuts. Before entering a deep winter sleep called torpor, they go through a phase called hyperphagia, where they eat up to 90 pounds of food a day to survive the cold months. .
